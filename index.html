Shilajit Guide

What makes our shilajit different

Pure shilajit is a mineral-rich resin that seeps from high Himalayan and Karakoram rocks in summer heat. Ours is collected at altitude, sun-purified using the traditional method, and lab-tested for purity — no fillers, no mixing, no shortcuts.

  • How to use: dissolve a pea-sized portion (about 300–500 mg) in warm milk or water, once daily — ideally in the morning.
  • Purity you can trust: every batch is lab-tested. Real shilajit dissolves completely and never leaves a gritty residue.
  • Storage: keep the jar cool and dry, away from sunlight. Use the small spoon inside — never a wet one.

Shilajit is a natural traditional product, not a medicine. If you are pregnant, nursing, or managing a health condition, please consult your doctor first.
